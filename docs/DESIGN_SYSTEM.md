# TSLPRB design system — "Brolly on cream"

Source of truth: `packages/design-tokens/tokens.json` (Tailwind preset + TS object via `src/index.ts`). This page explains intent. Rebranded 2026-09-05 (F-28) to Brolly Solutions' identity; the spec is `docs/specs/2026-09-05-brolly-rebrand-design.md`.

## Identity
Warm and restrained: cream surfaces, charcoal ink, gold spent only where it means something. One ink-filled primary action per screen; gold marks what the candidate did (answered, selected, active filter, progress); ink marks a deliberate flag (marked for review); red is wrong / missing / critical; green is eligible / correct. Soft radii (4–16 px), 1 px hairlines for structure, warm ink shadows for elevation. Playfair Display carries titles and the wordmark; Inter carries everything else; Noto Sans Telugu carries Telugu text and Noto Serif Telugu its titles. The umbrella lockup is the in-app brand mark; "TSLPRB" and "PWT" stay in copy as the exam's name.

## Spacing & shape
4-pt grid; screen padding 16; card padding 12–16; gaps 8–12. Radii: `xs` 4 (tags), `sm` 6 (buttons, cells, inputs), `md` 8 (cards, toasts), `lg` 12 (dialogs), `xl` 16 (sheet top corners), `full` (pills, toggle). Shadows are warm ink: `card` (`0 6px 20px -12px` at 25 %) under cards, `raised` under toasts, `sheet` (cast upward) under dialogs — the bottom sheet is a `@gorhom` surface and carries no shadow of its own; `shadowStyle(name)` returns the `boxShadow` object. Touch targets 48 (secondary) / 56 (primary, keypad 58). The brand rule (`Rail`) is 3 px solid gold and lives in the dev gallery only; the attempt clock's critical state is the static 4 px `HeaderBand` instead (Phase D).

Pressed feedback on cream is a `surface2` fill (`pressedClass`), never opacity — opacity is invisible between two creams. Filled controls (ink, gold) dim to 85 % instead (`pressedStyle`). A resting fill and the pressed fill share one class slot so two `bg-*` utilities never compete.

## Screen patterns (apps/mobile/src/ui)
Six compositions of the primitives above. A screen reaches for these before it writes a layout
of its own; each has a section in the dev gallery (`/dev/states`, section 12). P2 is not new:
the surface card the patterns sit in is `Card`, listed with the primitives above.

**The pattern API.** Every one of them takes an optional `className` (merged last, so a caller
can adjust layout — margins, flex — without forking the piece) and an optional `testID` (which
names its own inner parts: `<id>-row`, `<id>-mark`, `<id>-end`, `<id>-dot`, `<id>-retry`).
Nothing else is optional-by-omission: a pattern's other props describe its content, and a
caller that needs to override identity — a colour, a fill — is telling you the variant set is
missing something. A pattern is never a control unless the table says so.

| # | Pattern | Props | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | `Pill` | `label?` (a string, or a `<Trans>` that carries a `<Num>`), `tone` `quiet\|gold\|ink`, `align` `start\|center`, `dot`, `dotTone` `gold\|danger\|ok\|none`, `leading` | The label that names a block, a state or a step: a capsule with a minimum height (never fixed), a caption-size tracked label and an optional 6 px dot. `quiet` is `surface2` under a hairline with `ink3`; `gold` is the gold tint under `accentStrong` with ink; `ink` is the ink fill with cream. **A dot means status — mine, active, failed, passed — never decoration**, so a heading that only names a block takes `dotTone="none"` (or no dot at all). `align` routes through `dir()`, so the reading edge follows the language. Never a control — a pressable filter is `Chip shape="pill"`. |
| P1 | `PageHeader` | `trailing`, `pill`, `title`, `subtitle`, `titleTestID` | The hub opening: whatever sits beside the title at the reading end (the language switcher, a sign-in capsule), then a pill, then the screen's name in the display face. 12 px under the lockup, 8 px inside the pill–title–subtitle block. |
| P1 | `BackHeader` | `title?`, `onBack`, `trailing`, `children` | The leaf bar: a 48 px chevron-back target and an Inter 600 one-line title on `surface` under a `line`, with a slot after the title and a second row beneath. `title` is optional (Phase E, D17): the topic reader's own title is a display-face line in its body that may wrap to two, so its bar carries only the chevron and the language switcher, and a `flex-1` spacer takes the title's slot so `trailing` still sits at the far edge. |
| P3 | `StatTile` | `value?`, `label`, `empty`, `align` | One number and what it counts: a `surface2` tile, `<Num variant="stat">` in ink over an `ink3` caption, an em dash in `ink3` when there is nothing yet, read as one node. |
| P4 | `MarkerRow` | `title`, `titleTestID`, `titleLines`, `meta`, `metaTestID`, `marker` `none\|dot\|done\|locked`, `trailing`, `trailingLabel`, `chevron`, `onPress`, `first`, `accessibilityLabel` | The list row inside a card: a gold dot (to do), an ink `checkmark-circle` (done) or an `ink3` `lock-closed-outline`, an ink 600 title over an `ink3` line, a trailing pill or chevron, a hairline on top and a 56 px floor that grows with the label. `meta` takes a node as well as a string, for the two things a string cannot carry — digits, which live in `<Num>`/`Measure`, and a sentence that needs its own colour (Affairs' summary). A node is **silent in the composed name**, and only a PRESSABLE row composes one, so the props are a union rather than a rule: a node meta on a button requires an `accessibilityLabel`, a node meta on a static row needs none, because nothing is composed and the children are read as they come. `titleTestID`/`metaTestID` let a screen keep the IDs it already had (Affairs' headline and summary) while the pattern keeps the typography. `titleLines` caps the title (left off, it grows). It sits outside the body's ScrollView and **owns the bottom safe-area inset** — its host passes `Screen bottomInset={false}`, the way a tab scene does. `bordered={false}` (the Welcome footer) drops the line and the shadow together, and `grow={false}` (the attempt footer) lets a fixed-width primary hug its own 112 px so the remaining width goes to the secondary slot rather than to a wrapper the button cannot grow into. |
| P6 | `EmptyState` / `Skeleton` / `LoadError` | `message`, `title`/`action`/`dotTone`/`blocks`/`onRetry` | The three waiting states, one family: a quiet pill over one `ink3` line (with an optional outline `action`); static `surface2` blocks in the shape of the content, hidden from screen readers; and the same 24 px pill with a `danger` dot over the reason and a 48 px outline retry. `EmptyState` and `LoadError` centre identically — a different badge on each read as two mechanisms — and `EmptyState dotTone` lets a not-found wear the same red dot, since an id nothing answers to is a failure of a sort. An empty shelf takes no dot: nothing has gone wrong. |

A single-choice card (the post step, the category grid) is `Card` with `selected`: it reports
`accessibilityRole="radio"` with a checked state, and its 2 px edge is `accentStrong` — the
brand `accent` measures 2.34:1 against the cream around it, under the 3:1 non-text floor. A
`Card` or `Chip` with no `onPress` announces no selection at all: a badge nobody can choose
saying "selected" is a lie. (`StatTile` still has no selectable variant: Phase C found no screen that selects a
tile, and a variant with no caller is a guess. The category grid stays a `Card`.)

`startEdge()` (from `src/ui/edge.ts`, moved out of `features/result/` in Phase E once `Card` and four screens imported it) draws the 3 px gold start edge on the one card
per screen that carries it, as an object style rather than a class — RN's `borderStartWidth`
follows `I18nManager`, not the in-app language. **The edge replaces the boundary that side
already had, so only the difference comes off the reading-side padding**, and the box that
owns both numbers does that sum: `Card startEdge` internally (two pixels over its 1 px `line`),
`startEdgeInset(isRTL, pad, borderWidth)` for the one site that is not a card — a paper option,
which draws no border and so gives back all three. A screen that subtracts the whole edge puts
its content a pixel inside its neighbours'. Icons are Ionicons (`checkmark-circle`,
`lock-closed-outline`, `cloud-offline-outline`); chevrons stay the `Glyph` `‹ ›` so they never
depend on the language face carrying the character.

## Audit component contracts — 9 September 2026

- Native Button uses minimum heights of 48/56px with vertical padding and wrapping labels; callers should avoid fixed height overrides. Palette columns respect the 48px minimum and grow with text. Large-text attempt navigation stacks within a scrollable footer bounded to 40% of the viewport.
- Native Dialog uses RN Modal with a safe-area-constrained ScrollView. It focuses its accessible title on show and accepts an explicit `onDismiss` for hardware Back. A missing cancellation callback never invokes a decision action. Native window isolation/focus restoration still require VoiceOver/TalkBack device verification.
- Native tabs use wrapping custom labels and allocate extra height for enlarged text. Profile rows compose the field and selected value into one accessible name.
- Web `.topic-grid` cards use 16px padding without decorative heading bars. Statistics use shared grid rows. Telugu h1–h3 have normal tracking and 1.6 line height; question text remains 1.8.
- Web exam navigation focuses the numbered question legend after a successful move. At narrow widths, the timer stays sticky and controls remain fixed above a measured content reserve. Failure notices stay visible while scrolling and offset the timer/header.
- Persisted web UI reads the shared storage status before claiming a save. Temporary-storage warnings offer retry, retain pending writes/deletions, and advise keeping the tab open. Demo/sample labels remain visible and accessible near their content.

## Status vocabulary
Gold = the candidate's own input or the active state · ink = a deliberate flag (marked) · red = wrong, unanswered, critical · green = eligible, correct (text `okInk`). Timer (Phase D, as built): normal `surface2` under a `line` with ink digits, ≤ 5 min an `accentSoft` fill under an `accentStrong` edge with **ink** digits, ≤ 60 s solid `dangerInk` with `onInk` digits (5.79:1) — never `danger` with cream (2.48:1). The gold state's digits are ink, not `accentInk`: `accentInk` on `accentSoft` measures **3.34:1**, inside AA for the 23 px numeral but under it for the 12 px label above it, and the fill is what carries the warning. The last-minute toast is the same `dangerInk` + cream pair; a locked section takes the ink `info` toast, because it is a fact about the paper rather than a warning about the clock. One gold-edged card per screen is the rule, met on every screen since Phase D (the hero, the worked example, the open notice, the paper's key, the score, the correct answer).

**A number's colour follows whose number it is, not its sign.** Result's "Negative marks" figure is `accentInk` (4.77:1 on the card) while the attempt screen's `−0.25` is `dangerInk`, and both are right: a retrospective figure is the candidate's own result whatever its sign — the marks they gave away, like the minutes they spent — and gold is what this app calls the candidate's own input. The attempt screen's red is not a result at all: it is the paper's marking scheme, a rule of the exam, and red is the penalty in it. Gold means mine; red means the scheme's, or wrong (fix wave 1, D7).

## Motion
Sheet 180 ms cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.3,1) from bottom; a dialog's scrim fades over 140 ms while its card rises over 180 on the same curve; toast 180 ms slide-down. All reduce to instant changes under reduced motion. Nothing on the attempt screen animates for the clock: the critical state is three static marks (`motion.pulse` and `Rail critical` survive for the dev gallery alone). Haptics: selection on option/keypad, success on submit, warning on the 5-min/1-min toasts.

## Platform adaptation
Android (primary): edge-to-edge, gold-tinted ripples, predictive back handled in test (exit dialog), adaptive icon = gold "B" on navy. iOS: safe-area aware, sheet grabber, the same 1024 mark. Web: `boxShadow` renders as CSS; `?lang=` overrides the stored language for captures. Identity identical on all three.
